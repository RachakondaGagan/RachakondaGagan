## 🚀 About Me

I am **Gagan Rachakonda**, a B.Tech Computer Science Engineering student
at **GITAM Deemed University, Visakhapatnam**. I build polished,
scalable, and practical software products with a strong focus on
**full-stack development, AI engineering, cloud-native systems, and
product quality**.

My engineering philosophy is simple: **solve real problems, ship clean
code, and design for scale from day one**. I enjoy working across the
stack, from intuitive interfaces to reliable backend services, AI
integrations, and deployment pipelines.

**Core interests:** Full-stack systems, AI-powered applications,
automation, system design, distributed systems, and product engineering.
